Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Bhadex Plugs.

What is Bhadex Plugs?

Bhadex Plugs is a digital marketplace offering selected subscriptions, social media services and digital tools.

How do I place an order?

Browse the marketplace, select your product, choose a package, enter your email and proceed to checkout.

How can I pay?

Available payment methods are displayed during the Paystack checkout process.

Can I pay with Mobile Money?

Mobile Money is supported in the checkout configuration. Availability depends on Paystack and your payment account.

How do I select a Netflix plan?

Select Netflix from the marketplace and choose Standard or Premium in the package selection area.

Do you offer Spotify Family and Duo?

Yes, the available Spotify options are listed in the package selector.

Can I purchase TikTok Coins?

Yes. Select TikTok Coins and choose from the available coin packages.

Can I choose a different TikTok Coins package?

Yes. Choose the package you want from the checkout dropdown before making payment.

Do you offer Canva Pro?

Canva Pro is listed in our Editing, Design and AI Tools category.

Can I order CapCut Pro?

Yes. Check the marketplace for its available subscription option.

What happens after payment?

Keep your payment reference. Contact customer support with your reference and selected product for order assistance. Payment verification should be completed before delivery.

What if my payment is unsuccessful?

Check your payment status before trying again. If money was deducted, contact Paystack or our support team with the transaction reference.

Can I contact Bhadex Plugs?

Yes. You can contact us through WhatsApp on 0552817566 or email bhadexplugs@gmail.com.

Are all subscriptions automatically renewed?

Subscription renewal depends on the specific product and package. Check the terms of the service before ordering.

Can I request a product that is not listed?

Contact our support team to ask whether a particular digital product can be made available.

Can I get a refund?

Refund requests are reviewed according to the product and applicable service terms. Contact support with your transaction reference.

How do I know if my order is confirmed?

Keep your payment reference and contact support to confirm payment verification and order status.

Is customer information protected?

Customer information should be handled securely and used only for legitimate order and support purposes.
